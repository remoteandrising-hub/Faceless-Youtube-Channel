# Script: "The Doorway Effect — Why You Forget Why You Walked Into a Room"

**Format:** Faceless animated explainer, applying the cross-channel hook formula from
`channel-analysis.md` (closest match: Kurzgesagt's zoom-structure + MinutePhysics's
one-clean-idea build + School of Life's "this is about you" framing).

**Runtime:** 5:00 (target) · **Blocks:** 30 × 10s hard-cut motion blocks (5 shots/block,
~2s each) · **Aspect:** 16:9 · **Resolution:** 1080p · **Voice:** one warm, measured
narrator throughout, Kurzgesagt/School-of-Life pacing (not SciShow-fast) · **Subtitles:**
clean, on.

**Visual style:** flat-design animation, bold flat-color character (a simple yellow
"thought" blob, no face detail, Kurzgesagt-adjacent), gradient backgrounds, constant gentle
motion, infographic overlays for the science beats.

**Through-line prop:** a single stylized **yellow door** that appears in every block —
opens, closes, multiplies into a hallway of doors at the turn, and finally appears as a
"filing cabinet" of doors at the payoff. It is the one object that ties the hook to the
kicker.

**Arc:** hook = block 1 · build = blocks 2–13 · turn = block 14 · build (implications) =
blocks 15–26 · payoff = blocks 27–30.

## Through-line check
Prop: **the yellow door** — introduced in block 1, restated as a science object in 5–17,
reframed as a filing cabinet at the turn (14/28), and resolved literally in the final shot
(30), closing the loop the hook opened.

## Sources (for the factual claims above — required before any real production run)
- Gabriel Radvansky & colleagues, University of Notre Dame — "walking through doorways
  causes forgetting" (the original doorway-effect memory studies, including the virtual-
  reality replication).
- Steven Smith — foundational context-dependent memory research (the underwater word-list
  study design referenced in block 23 is drawn from the classic Godden & Baddeley diving
  experiment).
- Jeffrey Zacks / Khena Swallow — event segmentation theory (why the mind chunks continuous
  experience into discrete "events" at situational boundaries).

## Production notes (for whoever renders this)
- Every block is written to the pipeline's own contract: 27–32 words, 5 timed 2s hard-cut
  shots, one VO line, no filler, shot size/angle varied every cut, ≤2 consecutive blocks per
  location before rotating.
- Characters (the blob) never lip-sync — narration is external throughout, blob only
  emotes/gestures, per standard faceless-explainer rules.
- Recommended voice: a measured, warm narrator (Kurzgesagt/School-of-Life pace, not
  SciShow-fast) — this script's sentence rhythm is written for ~2.9 words/second delivery.
