# Baseline: Existing Channel Video Analysis

Source video: https://youtu.be/vsCV1zsf-Ps — "Ade's Customer Care College" (final chapter
of a seven-part customer service interview-prep course, ~31 min screencast with
picture-in-picture presenter).

This is the channel's existing content baseline. The scene breakdown below was produced by
automated scene-by-scene analysis; the critique maps it against the cross-channel hook
formula in [`channel-analysis.md`](channel-analysis.md).

## Against the six-channel hook formula

**What already works**
- Warm, direct-address teaching tone (School of Life register).
- A clear arc with a real payoff (the sample-answer analysis).
- Specific CTA rather than a vague "like and subscribe."

**Gaps vs. the formula**
1. **Throat-clearing open.** "Welcome to..." is exactly what all six reference channels
   cut. Their first line is a claim the viewer can be wrong about. Formula-style open for
   this same content: *"Most people fail customer service interviews on a question they
   were sure they answered well."*
2. **Credentials before demonstration.** ~6 minutes of authority-building (5:11–11:10)
   sits before the best material. Reference channels demonstrate first (one killer sample
   answer inside 90 seconds) and drop credentials as asides.
3. **The strongest content arrives at 11:10.** The STAR-method/sample-answer block is the
   retention engine, but most cold viewers won't reach it. The fix: chop it into
   escalating mini-hooks and open the video with the most counterintuitive one.
4. **Runtime.** 31 minutes suits enrolled students; discovery-oriented YouTube wants the
   same expertise sliced into 5-minute single-idea videos (one question, one myth, one
   framework per video).

## Implication for the faceless channel

The channel's niche is **interview prep / customer-service careers**, not general science.
The faceless-explainer formula transfers directly — each video takes ONE interview belief
viewers hold with confidence, proves it wrong, and rebuilds it. First script in this
series: [`script-tell-me-about-yourself.md`](script-tell-me-about-yourself.md).
