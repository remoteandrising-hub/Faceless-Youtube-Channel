# Script: "'Tell Me About Yourself' Is Not a Question — Why Almost Everyone Answers It Wrong"

**Niche fit:** interview prep / customer-service careers — the channel's actual teaching
domain (see `video-analysis-baseline.md`). Built on the cross-channel formula in
`channel-analysis.md`: Veritasium's misconception-trap hook + MinutePhysics's
one-clean-idea build + School of Life's "this is about you" reassurance.

**Runtime:** 5:00 (target) · **Blocks:** 30 × 10s hard-cut motion blocks (5 shots/block,
~2s each) · **Aspect:** 16:9 · **Resolution:** 1080p · **Voice:** one warm, measured
narrator throughout · **Subtitles:** clean, on.

**Visual style:** same flat-design system as `script-doorway-effect.md` for channel
consistency — the yellow blob character (minimal dot eyes, never lip-syncs), gradient
backgrounds, infographic overlays. New recurring second character: a gray
"interviewer" blob with simple glasses.

**Through-line prop:** a stylized **golden spotlight** that appears in every block. In the
hook it blinds the candidate; through the build the candidate keeps pointing it at the
wrong thing (their own past); at the turn it swivels; in the payoff it lands where it
belonged all along — on the employer's problem. Gets its own asset so it never morphs.

**Arc:** hook = block 1 · build = blocks 2–13 · turn = block 14 · build (the rebuild) =
blocks 15–26 · payoff = blocks 27–30.

## Through-line check
Prop: **the golden spotlight** — blinds the candidate in block 1, stays wrongly aimed
through the first build, physically swivels at the turn (14), becomes the tool of each of
the three moves (18–23), and is handed back in the payoff — the final frame holds on it,
resolving the hook's "not actually a question" claim.

## Formula check (vs `channel-analysis.md`)
- **Flat claim hook, no throat-clearing:** block 1 states a checkable, wrong-sounding claim.
- **Misconception-first (Veritasium):** blocks 2–8 make the viewer recognize *their own*
  wrong answer before correcting it.
- **One idea, escalating build (MinutePhysics):** one question, one framework, layered.
- **Personal identification (School of Life):** "you've rehearsed the wrong answer your
  whole life."
- **Kicker echoes the hook:** block 30 loops the first line back with new meaning.

## Sources (for factual claims — confirm before production)
- Eye-tracking research on recruiter resume review time (~6–8 seconds initial scan;
  Ladders Inc. eye-tracking study).
- First-impression formation and confirmation bias in employment interviews (structured
  interview literature; e.g., Dougherty, Turban & Callender's work on interviewer first
  impressions and confirmatory behavior).
- STAR method as standard behavioral-interview answer structure (widely documented
  hiring-practice framework).

## Production notes
- Same pipeline contract as the doorway script: 27–32 words per block, 5 timed 2s
  hard-cut shots, one VO line per block, shot size/angle varied every cut, ≤2 consecutive
  blocks per location.
- Characters never lip-sync; narration is external. Speech bubbles are shown as GRAPHICS
  (text icons), never mouthed.
- Reuse the yellow blob + add the gray interviewer blob and the golden spotlight as new
  reusable assets, so this and future interview-prep videos stay on-model.
